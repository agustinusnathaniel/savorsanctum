Keep track of useful finds and send someone directly to the item you want to show them.

### New Features

- **Saved items** — bookmark items for later and find them again from the Saved filter. Your saved list stays on your device.

- **Shareable item links** — copy a link to any item and open it directly at the highlighted result, so shared recommendations are easier to find.

### Improvements

- **Better mobile actions** — saving and sharing are easier to reach on touch screens without covering the item content.

- **More dependable sharing** — shared links now open the intended item without carrying over your personal Saved filter.
